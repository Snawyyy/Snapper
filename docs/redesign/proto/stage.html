Covered: inventory #66 to #97 (32 of 32) and the doc's new stage items

Drawing

  • #66 Empty state: toolbar Demo, pick "No shot". Message plus Add shot.
  • #67 Double click Teto or the poster: red outline of the whole shape pops in.
  • #68 With Teto picked whole: yellow lean bar on the right (drag up/down), swivel bar below (drag sideways). They slide in.
  • #69 Click any piece: red outline plus yellow pivot dot at its joint (dotted stem to its turn handle).
  • #70 Head starts picked: faint grid lines and small yellow dots. Body has a grid too.
  • #71 Head top corner dots: yellow ring. Drag Teto or press Space: the dots lag, bounce, and show a tail on the side they trail.
  • #72 Pick Body: center dot (pulse) and collar dot (wave) have a yellow square that breathes in Full motion, still in Calm/Off. Play to see them move.
  • #73 Click a warp dot: red ring, dashed reach ellipse, label "reach 1.5".
  • #74 Any piece of Teto picked: small yellow squares at hands and feet.
  • #75 Drag on empty stage: dashed red box with light fill; things inside get the hover outline live; chip "3 picked".

Mouse

  • #76 Click a piece: picks it (red at once, nothing moves until 4 px). With Teto picked whole, a click keeps the whole doll.
  • #77 Shift+click: adds, outline pops; then drag moves them all.
  • #78 Ctrl+click adds and a drag scales; Ctrl+click a picked thing without dragging drops it (outline fades).
  • #79 Drag a picked thing: moves every pick; chip "+30, 0 px"; ghost at the start; one undo "Move Head".
  • #80 Shift+drag: locked to the stronger axis.
  • #81 Ctrl+drag: scales every pick; chip "x 1.20"; one undo "Scale ...".
  • #82 Lean bar drag: chip "lean 12 deg"; undo "Lean Teto".
  • #83 Swivel bar drag: chip "swivel 30 deg"; undo "Swivel Teto".
  • #84 Drag a warp dot: grid bends live, neighbours inside its reach follow; chip "4, -2 px"; undo "Warp Head".
  • #85 Click a warp dot without dragging: picks it (red ring, reach ring); click again drops it.
  • #86 Drag a hand or foot square: two bone bend for arms; chip "bend 40 deg"; undo "Bend left arm".
  • #87 Box select replaces; Shift adds (chip "+2"); Ctrl removes (chip "-1").
  • #88 Click empty space: pick clears, outlines fade.
  • #89 Double click a piece: picks the whole doll, bars slide in. Double click the poster: whole layer.
  • #90 Wheel over the stage turns the pick (up = counterclockwise), chip "-15 deg", one undo per burst. Nothing picked: chip "Click a piece first, then turn it." for 1.5 s, no shake.
  • #91 Shift+wheel: 1 deg a notch.
  • #92 Wheel with a warp dot picked: reach ring grows/shrinks, label updates; Shift is fine.
  • #93 Pick the poster, right click Teto's arm: "Link movement to Teto (whole)" and "Link movement to Left forearm". Red rope draws in 200 ms. Greyed with why when it cannot act; hover for the tooltip.
  • #94 Right click with a linked pick: "Unlink movement" (greyed with why when nothing picked is linked).

Keys

  • #95 Escape: cancels a drag (snaps back to the ghost in 150 ms); else drops the picked dot; else clears the pick.
  • #96 Ctrl+A: every piece of the focused doll, else every layer.
  • #97 D: toggles "drags behind" on the picked dot (ring appears); with none, chip "Click a warp dot of a picked piece first."

New in the design doc

  • F1 Hover outline: 1 px dashed white on what a click would grab.
  • F6 Value chip by the cursor on every drag and wheel.
  • G6 Handles say what they do: turn, lean, swivel, bend glyphs; cursor changes; hover grows 15 %. Toolbar "Show grab zones" draws the 12 px reach.
  • F9 F5 Space+drag or middle drag pans; Ctrl+wheel zooms at the cursor; Shift+1 fit, Shift+2 zoom to pick; zoom combo.
  • F10 Guides: drag the poster or Teto near the shot centre or another thing's edge. Red line, white flash on catch, 2 px settle on drop.
  • Link rope Red rope from follower to leader, drawn in 200 ms, shown while the follower is picked.
  • F16 G13 Demo "No doll": drag the Teto chip onto the stage, or press the button. Doll squashes 5 % and springs back.
  • F3 Stage focused: arrows nudge 1 px, Shift 10 px; Alt+arrows turn 1 deg, Alt+Shift 15 deg. No pick: arrows step frames.
  • F18 F20 Tab steps pieces, Shift+Tab back; Enter dives from the whole doll into its head.
  • Undo Things flash and slide back in 120 ms; toasts with Undo on add, delete, link, unlink.
  • UX Double click empty space adds a sticker (pop and ring); Delete removes picked layers (puff, toast with Undo); deleting a piece is refused with the reason. Right click a warp dot changes its mode: Still, Drags behind, Wave, Pulse.
  • Play Space, transport buttons or the frame slider (snaps to ticks). Onion skin (O) shows frames -2 and +2.
