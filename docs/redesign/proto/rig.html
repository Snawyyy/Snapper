Rig tab

Rig a doll by touching it. Click a piece, drag its yellow joint to move the pivot (it catches the piece centre and edges), drag the small rope hook onto the part it hangs from, drag rows in Stack view to restack. Pick the Skirt and click a grid dot: D makes it drag behind, the wheel grows its reach, a Moves tile animates it and its neighbours follow. Ctrl+Z undoes any of it by name.
