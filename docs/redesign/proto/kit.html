Number fields .num .num-label .num-input

Canvas: hover, drag, snap, chip Kit.drag Kit.snapper Kit.hoverOutline

Hover a piece: dashed outline. Click picks it (red), Shift adds. Drag: nothing moves until 4 px, then it jumps to the cursor, a ghost holds the start and a chip says how far. Centres and edges catch within 6 px (red guide flashes, the piece settles with a 2 px bounce). Shift locks the axis. The yellow handle turns it; Shift snaps 15 deg. Escape mid-drag snaps back. Double-click empty space adds a piece; Delete removes; arrows nudge 1 px, Shift 10.
