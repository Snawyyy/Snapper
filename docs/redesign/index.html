Snapper: Win98, but juicier

A redesign of how Snapper looks and answers your hands. The dark Win98 bevels and Teto red stay. On top: things pop, settle, shake when blocked, and every drag tells you its number. No manager, model or file format changes. Widgets only, plus three small pure helpers with tests.

1. Principles

  1. Answer on the same frame Something changes the frame you press. Press to feedback under 50 ms, never over 100 ms. A fast click still shows 1 frame of the down state.
    G1, G5
  2. One motion table Press 80 ms, hover 100 ms, pop 150 ms, move 200 ms, cap 300 ms. Hover and moves ease out cubic. Pops ease out back, 10 % overshoot: cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1). Nothing linear.
    G20, G8, G22
  3. Look small, grab big Handles drawn 8 px, grabbed within 12 px (3x). Nearest wins. A drag starts after 4 px, then jumps to the cursor. Hidden handles stay grabbable 100 ms.
    G2, G3, G15
  4. Every drag talks A value chip rides 14 px off the cursor ("34 deg", "+3 f"). A faint ghost marks the start. Snaps catch within 6 px, the target flashes 100 ms, the thing settles with a 2 px bounce.
    F6, G4, G17
  5. Show what changed New keys pop 1.4 to 1.0 in 150 ms with a red ring. Deletes puff out in 120 ms. Undo flashes and slides back in 120 ms. Big actions get a 4 s toast with Undo.
    G7, G9, G11, F15
  6. Never fail in silence A blocked click shakes the control 4 px for 200 ms and shows the reason next to it. Greyed controls keep their tooltip.
    G18, UX language
  7. Small often, big rare Routine edits: at most 150 ms, no shake, no sound. Bursts and hit-stop (70 ms) only for export done, rig saved, first doll on stage. Motion: Full, Calm, Off; Calm drops overshoot, Off drops all of it.
    G10, G16, G21, G22
  8. Hands over numbers Drag a label to scrub (1 per px, Shift 10, Alt 0.1). Type math ("*2", "90/2"). Arrows nudge 1 px, Shift 10 px. Fields are the backup, not the way in.
    F2, F3, F7

12. Build order

Small steps, each one shippable on its own branch, best value first. Each leaves its tests in tests/ui or the module it touches, and updates ARCHITECTURE.md when it adds a piece.

  1. Motion table and Motion switch (G20, G21). One place for durations and curves; Full, Calm, Off in the View menu, saved in QSettings, Calm by default when the system asks for less motion. Every later step reads it.
    theme.h, theme.cpp, new ui/motion.h/.cpp (runs a QVariantAnimation from the table), main_window.cpp, file_menu.cpp
  2. Scrub labels and math in fields (F2, F7, G3). A ScrubLabel next to every number field; the drag opens an EditScope so it is one undo step, Escape cancels. Math is a pure function with tests, not widget logic.
    new ui/scrub_label.h/.cpp, form_helpers.cpp, keyed_boxes.cpp, layer_box.cpp, drag_box.cpp, link_box.cpp, rig_panel_motion.cpp; new base/number_text.h (EvalNumber) and tests/base
  3. Hover, grab big, dead zone (F1, G2, G3, G6, G15). Hover outline and handle grow on the stage and rig canvas; 12 px grab, nearest wins; 4 px before any drag; cursors per handle.
    pose_tool.h/.cpp, pose_tool_handles.cpp, stage_view.cpp, rig_canvas.cpp, rig_canvas_input.cpp, timeline_input.cpp, reel_timeline_input.cpp, shot_strip.cpp
  4. Toasts and error shake (F15, G18). Problem signals go to a toast; big actions get Undo in the toast; the refusing control shakes.
    new ui/toast.h/.cpp, main_window.cpp, problem.h, inspector.cpp, video_page.cpp
  5. Drags talk (F6, G4, G17). Value chip, start ghost, snap flash and settle on stage, timeline, reel and strip.
    stage_view.cpp, pose_tool.cpp, timeline_paint.cpp, timeline_view.cpp, reel_timeline.cpp, shot_strip.cpp
  6. Keys pop, puff and rewind (G7, G9, G11, G19). Which keys came or went is worked out by a pure helper that diffs two project values, with tests; views only animate it.
    timeline_paint.cpp, timeline_view.cpp, reel_timeline.cpp, playhead.cpp; new edit/change_diff.h and tests/edit
  7. Top bar: chunky tabs, transport, named Undo/Redo buttons, press states on custom buttons (G5).
    main_window.cpp, theme.cpp (QProxyStyle press offset), file_menu.cpp
  8. Timeline key chip bar and corner switch. Same KeyManager calls as the right-click menu.
    timeline_view.cpp, timeline_input.cpp, timeline_paint.cpp, timeline_layout.h
  9. Inspector reshape (F14, F8): title line, dials, motion tiles, spring preview, folding sections, Mixed.
    inspector.cpp, keyed_boxes.cpp, motion_box.cpp, drag_box.cpp, dot animation box (warp-animations branch), link_box.cpp
  10. Stage zoom, pan, fit, guides, nudge (F9, F5, F10, F3, F18, F20). Guide snapping is pure math in anim with tests.
    stage_view.h/.cpp, pose_tool.h/.cpp, rig_canvas.cpp, stage_menu.cpp; new anim/guides.h and tests/anim
  11. Cards and drag-in: cast cards and drag to stage, shot strip chips and slides, reel film cards, cut tabs, transition badges, inline slot picker (G13).
    cast_panel.cpp, cast_actions.cpp, shot_strip.cpp, shot_bin.cpp, reel_timeline.cpp, reel_timeline_drop.cpp, slot_picker.cpp, video_page.cpp

What stays the same

  • Every manager in edit (HistoryManager, PoseManager, KeyManager, PlaybackManager, DollLibraryManager and the rest) and the one edit path: widget calls manager, manager calls Apply.
  • EditScope drags: one undo step per drag, Escape cancels.
  • model, anim, render, io, media: untouched, except three new pure helpers with tests (number math, change diff, guide snap).
  • File formats, the right-click menus and all keys that exist today. New gestures are added on top; nothing is taken away, except the shot transition controls (strip menu, Into next, Overlap), which move to cuts in the Video tab.
  • Wheel turn stays 5 deg, Shift 1 deg. The 15 deg Shift snap is for the turn handle drag only, so the two do not clash.
